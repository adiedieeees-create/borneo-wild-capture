BORNEO WILD CAPTURE — ITINERARY PAGE 02 — V8

PACKAGE CONTENTS
- index.html: homepage and primary trip information sections.
- itinerary.html: Page 02; how-to-get-here.html: Page 03, designed in the Slate & Champagne palette.
- assets/: local wildlife photographs, supplied sunset-boat hero image, and shared site.js behavior.

DESIGN DIRECTION
- Homepage cover retains its warm, sunset/orangutan visual identity.
- Inner sections and Page 02 use deep slate/teal, champagne gold, and ivory.
- Brand mark, wordmark, buttons, navigation, and typography are kept consistent.
- Layout adapts to desktop and mobile widths.

INTERACTIONS
- Main navigation links move to their matching sections or pages.
- Mobile hamburger menus open and close; they also close after selecting a link, clicking outside, pressing Escape, or resizing to desktop.
- WhatsApp trip enquiry links use the official Borneo Wild Capture number: +62 851-9083-6669.
- Instagram and email contact links are included.
- Wildlife gallery thumbnails are keyboard- and touch-accessible; select a photo to open the full-size lightbox. Close with the close control, Escape, or by selecting the backdrop.
- Hotel cards and map/airline links open their relevant external destinations.
- Remote hotel images have a designed visual fallback if the external image host is unavailable.

LOCAL PHOTOS
All nine wildlife images used in the homepage gallery/sections are included as local WebP files. The Page 02 sunset-boat hero image is included locally at assets/DSCF0061.jpg. The hotel card photos remain remote because the supplied source package did not include local hotel photographs; visual fallbacks are provided if they fail to load.

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP while preserving the folder structure.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. Use the navigation or the homepage How to Get Here link to open Page 02.
4. Open the Gallery section and tap any photo to view it at full size.
5. Test the mobile menu at phone width.

SCOPE AND DEPLOYMENT
This package contains the Home page, the new standalone Itinerary page, and the How to Get Here page. It does not publish or deploy the website and does not modify GitHub, hosting, DNS, or paid services. Confirm flight schedules, hotel availability, and transfers before travel.
