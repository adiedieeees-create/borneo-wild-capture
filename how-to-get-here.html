02 / JOURNEY

How to
Get Here.

Plan your journey to Tanjung Puting National Park — from your arrival in Pangkalan Bun to the quiet waterways of the rainforest.

Flights, stays
and the river route.

Tanjung Puting is reached through Pangkalan Bun and Kumai. Fly into Iskandar Airport (PKN), arrange your onward road transfer, then continue by traditional klotok along the Sekonyer River into the national park.

ARRIVING IN CENTRAL BORNEO

Flight information

Choose a flight to Iskandar Airport (PKN), Pangkalan Bun. Flight routes and schedules change, so confirm current availability with the airline before travel.

Fly to Pangkalan Bun

Iskandar Airport (PKN) is the air gateway for visitors heading to Tanjung Puting. Arrange airport pickup in advance, especially when connecting to a same-day boat departure.

THE JOURNEY CONTINUES

From airport to rainforest.

There is no airport inside Tanjung Puting National Park. The journey combines a flight, a road transfer and a river expedition.

01 / ARRIVE

Iskandar Airport (PKN)

Land in Pangkalan Bun, Central Kalimantan.

02 / ROAD

Kumai

Continue by pre-arranged car to the agreed tourist boat jetty.

03 / RIVER

Sekonyer River

Board your klotok and travel upriver through the rainforest.

04 / EXPLORE

Tanjung Puting

Visit park destinations with your guide and boat crew.
