Flights, accommodation,
and directions.

Tanjung Puting National Park is located in Central Kalimantan (Borneo), Indonesia. The main gateway is Pangkalan Bun (PKN), with direct flights from Jakarta, comfortable hotel options, and onward transfers to the boat departure point in Kumai.

Flight Information

Fly to Pangkalan Bun (PKN)
The nearest airport to Tanjung Puting National Park.

Batik AirDIRECT FLIGHT
Jakarta (CGK) → Pangkalan Bun (PKN)

Approx. 1 hr 25 min · Flight times vary by date

NAM AirDIRECT FLIGHT
Jakarta (CGK) → Pangkalan Bun (PKN)

Approx. 1 hr 15 min · Flight times vary by date

Direct services are currently listed by Batik Air and NAM Air. Flight schedules and availability can change; confirm your travel date with the airline before booking.
