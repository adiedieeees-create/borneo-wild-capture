How to Get Here

Plan your journey to Tanjung Puting
National Park with ease.

From the first flight into Pangkalan Bun to the quiet waterways of the Sekonyer River, every step brings you closer to the heart of Borneo.

Central Kalimantan · Indonesia

Flights, accommodation,
and directions.

Tanjung Puting National Park is located in Central Kalimantan, Indonesian Borneo. The main gateway is Pangkalan Bun, served by Iskandar Airport (PKN). From there, continue by road to Kumai Harbour, where your river expedition begins aboard a traditional klotok boat.

The final stretch

From runway
to rainforest.

After landing at Iskandar Airport, continue by car to Kumai Harbour. From the harbour, board your klotok and travel along the Sekonyer River into Tanjung Puting National Park.

The drive from Iskandar Airport to Kumai is commonly planned at around 20 minutes. From Kumai, the klotok journey to Camp Leakey takes approximately 3 hours; actual timing varies with the destination, boat and river conditions.
